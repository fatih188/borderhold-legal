This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Border Hold (com.bsoftgame.bolderhold), developed by BsoftGame, and explains how information is handled when you play the game.

We do not require you to provide personal contact details such as your name, home address, phone number, or password to play Border Hold.   • Device & System Info: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, language/locale settings, and app version code.

  • Analytics & Advertising Identifiers: Advertising or analytics identifiers used for measurement, ad delivery, and fraud prevention where applicable.

2. How Information Is Used

Processed information is used to:

  • Comply with applicable laws and prevent abuse.

3. Third-Party Services

Border Hold may integrate third-party services such as Google Play Services and Google Analytics. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information and retain diagnostic or analytics data only for as long as needed for operational and service purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

Border Hold is handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws and Google Play policies. Parents or guardians with questions about data associated with a child's device can reach out through the developer contact channels listed on Google Play.
